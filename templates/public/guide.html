How to use AGRI SMART AI

Follow these simple steps to set up your farm workspace and start using decision-support tools.

1

Create your account

Register, then add your state and district in Profile. This helps with future scheme and weather matching.

2

Add your farm

Open My Farm and enter land area, soil type, irrigation method, location, and current crop.

3

Check weather

Open Weather. It uses demo data until you add OPENWEATHER_API_KEY to your .env file.

4

Use recommendations

Enter soil and climate data in Crop Recommendation. The result is clearly labelled ML or demo fallback.

5

Track farm finances

Add expenses to see your category and monthly graphs, then use Profit Prediction to plan a crop budget.

6

Monitor IoT later

Attach a device to a farm. AGRI SMART AI will compare moisture to that farm’s crop and show high/low-water alerts.
