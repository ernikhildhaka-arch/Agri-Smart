ABOUT AGRI SMART AI

Technology that respects practical farming.

AGRI SMART AI is a decision-support platform designed to help farmers organise their field data, review planning estimates, and prepare for future connected agriculture.

Our purpose

To make useful farm-planning tools accessible, understandable, and easy to use on any screen.

How we work

We separate advisory information, calculated estimates, and development-mode demonstrations so farmers understand what they are seeing.

Future direction

Verified schemes, local ML models, weather feeds, APIs, and IoT gateways can extend the platform without changing the farmer experience.
